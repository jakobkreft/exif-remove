Two new per-template options, for data a photo needs to look right:

* HDR gain map — keep it and an HDR photo still renders as HDR. The map
  holds no identifying data, and the block describing it is rebuilt from
  scratch, so none of the camera's own XMP comes along.
* Orientation — keep it and photos never show up sideways.

Camera info can now be randomized instead of left blank.

Colour profiles are cleaned too: one hid a maker's name as text that a
byte scan could not see.
