Two new per-template options for data a photo needs to look right:

* HDR gain map — keep it and an HDR photo still renders as HDR. The map
  holds no identifying data, and the block describing it is rebuilt from
  scratch so none of the camera's own XMP can come along.
* Orientation — keep it and photos never show up sideways. It says nothing
  about you.

Camera info can now be randomized, replacing the make and model with a
common handset instead of leaving a conspicuous blank.
